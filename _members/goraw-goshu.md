Dr. Goraw Goshu is the Director of the Blue Nile Water Institute (BNWI) at Bahir Dar University and the Principal Investigator of YeAbay Wuha Intel.
His expertise is in hydrogeology and water resources management.
He leads the project's scientific direction and its engagement with government and stakeholders.
