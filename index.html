Groundwater intelligence for the Abay Basin

YeAbay Wuha Intel is a 24-month applied research initiative led by the Blue Nile Water Institute at Bahir Dar University. It builds an AI-enabled, GPS-based platform that helps water officers and planners decide where to drill and how much groundwater can be sustained.

Highlights

Our Research

We combine satellite observations, aquifer testing, and machine learning to map groundwater potential and estimate sustainable yield.

See our research

Our Projects

The project builds a spatial groundwater database, an AI model, a groundwater flow model, and a web application, followed by field validation.

Our Team
