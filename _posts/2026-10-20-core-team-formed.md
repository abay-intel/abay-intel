Bahir Dar University has established the core technical team for YeAbay Wuha Intel, a 24-month applied research initiative led by the Blue Nile Water Institute (BNWI).
The team is led by Dr. Goraw Goshu as Principal Investigator, with Dr. Walelign Tewabe Sewunetie, Dr. Getnet Demil, and Dr. Sissay Asres as Co-PIs.
The team's establishment took effect on 12 October 2026.
